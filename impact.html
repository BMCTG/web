Impact Areas

Where We Make a Difference

Four pillars driving sustainable change for the Deaf and Hard of Hearing community.

📚

Education Impact

Empowering Deaf individuals with digital literacy, vocational training, and essential life skills to thrive in a connected world.

  • ✦ Digital skills training for youth & adults
  • ✦ Sign language literacy workshops
  • ✦ Financial literacy sessions
🏆

Sports Achievement

Building athletic excellence through dedicated training programs and competitive opportunities for Deaf athletes.

  • ✦ 25+ athletes trained across disciplines
  • ✦ Tournaments participated at district & state levels
  • ✦ Medals won in Deaf sports competitions
🌍

Community Engagement

Fostering a strong, connected community through inclusive events and meaningful partnerships that uplift every member.

  • ✦ Cultural events & social gatherings
  • ✦ Families reached through outreach
  • ✦ Partnerships with NGOs & government bodies
🗣️

Awareness Programs

Breaking barriers through advocacy, education, and collaboration — making inclusion a lived reality across Gujarat.

  • ✦ Schools visited with ISL awareness drives
  • ✦ Corporate inclusion training sessions
  • ✦ Government policy advocacy meetings
