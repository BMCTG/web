Partners

Partner With Us

We believe in the power of collaboration. Whether you are a corporation fulfilling CSR goals, a school looking to create inclusive spaces, or an individual who wants to support our cause — there is a partnership model that works for you.

🏢

Corporate CSR

Fulfil your corporate social responsibility goals while creating meaningful impact. Partner with BMCTG to fund education, sports and welfare programmes for the deaf and mute community. All contributions are eligible under Section 80G.

🏫

School Partnerships

Create inclusive learning environments by partnering with us. We provide sign-language training, deaf culture workshops and accessibility consultation to help schools welcome every student.

🏛️

Government Programs

Collaborate with BMCTG on government-led disability welfare and inclusion initiatives. We work closely with state and local bodies to expand the reach of public programmes for the deaf community.

❤️

Individual Supporters

Whether through monthly contributions, in-kind donations or offering your professional skills, individual supporters are the backbone of our mission. Every effort counts.
