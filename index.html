Our culture is our strength

Deaf culture is a rich and vibrant identity built on shared experiences, visual communication, and community bonds. At BMCTG, we celebrate this identity and promote awareness and inclusion throughout Gujarat.

Visual Communication

Sign language and visual cues are central to how we connect and express ourselves.

Community Bond

Strong ties within the deaf community create a deep sense of belonging and support.

Cultural Pride

We embrace deaf identity as a source of strength, pride, and resilience.

Equal Access

Everyone deserves access to information, services, education, and opportunity.
