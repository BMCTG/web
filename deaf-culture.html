Our Culture

Deaf Culture is Our Identity

Deaf culture is a vibrant, living culture with its own language, traditions, history, and community. It is not defined by what Deaf people cannot do, but by the richness of what they can. At BMCTG, we celebrate and nurture this identity every day — honouring the shared experiences, values, and bonds that make the Deaf community in Gandhinagar and Gujarat strong, proud, and united.

🤟 Indian Sign Language

Indian Sign Language — The Language of Our Community

ISL is a rich, visual-gestural language used by millions of Deaf and hard-of-hearing Indians. It has its own grammar, vocabulary, and syntax — completely distinct from spoken Hindi, Gujarati, or English. At BMCTG, we champion ISL as the primary language of education, communication, and cultural identity for the Deaf community. Promoting ISL means promoting equality, access, and dignity for every Deaf individual in Gujarat and across India.

🗣️

Language

ISL is a full, living language with its own structure. We teach, preserve, and promote Indian Sign Language in schools, workplaces, and community spaces throughout Gandhinagar.

📖

Identity

The Deaf Community is a Linguistic Minority

Deaf people are not defined by hearing loss — they are defined by their language, their culture, and their community. Deaf identity is a source of pride. When Deaf individuals come together through ISL, they form a linguistic and cultural minority with a shared heritage, shared values, and a shared vision for an inclusive world. At BMCTG, we believe every Deaf person deserves to be proud of who they are, to be seen and heard in their own language, and to participate fully in every aspect of society.

✊

Deaf Pride

Deaf identity is not a disability — it is a cultural identity. We celebrate being Deaf as a source of strength and belonging.

Sign Language

Indian Sign Language (ISL)

Indian Sign Language is the primary language of the Deaf community in India. ISL is a complete, natural language with its own grammar, syntax, and regional variations — it is not a sign system based on spoken languages. ISL connects millions of Deaf Indians and is the foundation of Deaf culture, education, and identity. BMCTG works to promote ISL through education, awareness, training, and community events in Gandhinagar and across Gujarat.

🤟

Complete Language

ISL has its own grammar, vocabulary, and structure — fully independent of spoken languages used in India.

📡

Visual Communication

ISL relies on handshapes, facial expressions, and body language — a rich visual system of expression.

🏫

Education & Access

ISL interpretation in schools, courts, hospitals, and government services ensures equal access for Deaf citizens.

🇮🇳

National Recognition

ISL was recognised by the RPWD Act 2016. BMCTG continues to advocate for full implementation and ISL awareness.
