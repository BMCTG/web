Training Programs

In-depth sports programs

Each sport has dedicated coaching, regular training sessions, and pathways to competitive tournaments at district, state and national levels.

🏏

Cricket Program

Our cricket program is one of the most popular sports initiatives at BMCTG. We train deaf athletes in batting, bowling, and fielding techniques while fostering teamwork and sportsmanship through regular practice sessions and inter-club friendly matches.

Football Program

Deaf football players communicate through sign language on the pitch, creating a unique and powerful form of teamwork. Our football program offers structured training in passing, dribbling, and tactical awareness, preparing teams for inter-state deaf football tournaments.

Training Coaches Players Tournaments

🏸

Badminton Program

Badminton is one of the fastest-growing sports in the deaf community of Gujarat. Our program covers singles and doubles techniques, footwork drills, and match strategy. Players train regularly and participate in local and state-level deaf badminton championships.

Athletics Program

Our athletics program covers sprinting, long-distance running, relay races, and field events like long jump and shot put. Athletes train rigorously and represent Gujarat at national deaf sports meets, building endurance, speed, and competitive spirit.

Swimming Program

Swimming promotes physical fitness, confidence, and water safety for deaf athletes. Our program offers coaching in freestyle, backstroke, and breaststroke, with opportunities to compete in district and state-level deaf swimming competitions.

Chess Program

Chess is a game of strategy that transcends hearing. Our deaf chess players compete at state, national, and international levels with remarkable focus and tactical skill. We provide coaching for beginners through advanced players and organize regular club tournaments.

Volleyball Program

Volleyball builds teamwork, reflexes, and athletic ability among deaf athletes. Our program focuses on serving, spiking, setting, and defensive techniques, with regular practice sessions leading up to inter-community and state-level deaf volleyball tournaments.

Table Tennis Program

Table tennis develops quick reflexes, hand-eye coordination, and precision. Our deaf table tennis players train in forehand and backhand techniques, spin variations, and match tactics. The program encourages participation from youth and adults in local and state competitions.
