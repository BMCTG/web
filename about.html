About BMCTG

Building a stronger Deaf community since 2015

Our Story

A community-led initiative for lasting change

Badhir Mandal Charitable Trust (BMCTG) was founded by Jaydeepsinh Dabhi, a deaf individual who experienced firsthand the challenges faced by the deaf and mute community in Gujarat. In 2015, he started with just a few families in Gandhinagar, driven by a simple belief: every deaf person deserves access to opportunity, education, and community.

What began as a grassroots effort has grown into a recognized charitable trust serving hundreds of individuals and families across Gandhinagar and Gujarat. Today, BMCTG runs education programs, sports initiatives, employment support, women’s empowerment, and Indian Sign Language interpretation services — all designed by and for the deaf community.

✓
Community Led — Founded by a deaf person, governed by deaf leaders, and driven by community needs.
✓
Holistic Approach — Addressing education, employment, health, sports, and social inclusion together.
✓
ISL Interpretation — Providing Indian Sign Language interpreters for government services, healthcare, and education.

Mission

What we stand for

Our mission and vision guide every program, every initiative, and every life we touch.

🎯

Our Mission

To empower the deaf and mute community through education, sports, skills development, and Indian Sign Language interpretation — creating equal access to opportunity, dignity, and participation in every aspect of society.

🔭

Our Vision

A Gujarat — and an India — where deaf individuals are fully included, their sign language is respected, their talents are celebrated, and no barrier stands between them and a fulfilling, independent life.

Our Journey

A decade of community impact

From a handful of families to a recognized trust — our milestones tell the story of collective determination.

2015

Founded in Gandhinagar

Jaydeepsinh Dabhi starts BMCTG with a few families, driven by a vision of community empowerment.

2017

Sports Program Launched

Cricket, football, badminton and more — deaf athletes begin training and competing at the state level.

2019

Deaf School Initiative

BMCTG partners with schools to create deaf-friendly classrooms and provide ISL-trained educators.

2021

Employment Support

Vocational training and job placement programs launch, connecting deaf individuals with inclusive employers.

2023

Women’s Empowerment Program

Dedicated programs for deaf women — covering health, skills, leadership, and economic independence.

2025

Expansion & Growth

BMCTG expands across Gujarat with new programs, partnerships, and a growing community of 400+ individuals served.
